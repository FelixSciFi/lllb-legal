# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

_Last updated / 最后更新: 2026-05-05_

## English

### What we collect
**Nothing on our servers.** LLLB has no backend. The developer cannot see
your data because we never receive it.

The following data lives **only on your device** and (optionally) in
**your own iCloud account**:
- Learning progress (which sentences played, total listening time, candy
  balance, achievements)
- App preferences (selected languages, voice settings, playback speed)
- Subscription / promo state

iCloud Key-Value Storage is used only to sync your own progress between
your own Apple devices. We do not have access to that data.

### What we don't do
- No analytics SDK (no Google Analytics, no Firebase, no third-party trackers)
- No advertising
- No email or contact collection
- No selling of any data — there's nothing to sell

### Third parties
The only third party involved is **Apple**, for in-app purchases (StoreKit)
and iCloud sync. Apple's privacy policy applies to those services.

### Children
LLLB is not directed at children under 13. The App is rated 9+ on the
App Store and is intended for general audiences.

### Data deletion
Delete the App to remove all on-device data. To clear iCloud-synced
progress, sign out of iCloud or remove the App's iCloud data in
**Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage**.

### Changes
We may update this policy. Continued use after an update means you accept
the new policy.

### Contact
Questions: **sduzfan@gmail.com**

## 中文

### 我们收集什么
**服务器上什么都没有。** LLLB 没有后端，开发者看不到你的数据，因为
数据从未发送给我们。

以下数据**仅存储在你的设备**和（可选）**你自己的 iCloud 账户**中：
- 学习进度（播放过的句子、累计时长、candy 余额、成就）
- App 偏好（语言选择、语音设置、播放速度）
- 订阅 / 礼包状态

iCloud 键值存储（KVS）仅用于在你自己的 Apple 设备之间同步进度。我们
无法访问这些数据。

### 我们不做的事
- 不接任何分析 SDK（没有 Google Analytics、Firebase、第三方追踪器）
- 不投放广告
- 不收集邮箱或联系方式
- 不出售任何数据——也没数据可卖

### 第三方
唯一涉及的第三方是 **Apple**，用于内购（StoreKit）和 iCloud 同步。
这两项服务适用 Apple 的隐私政策。

### 儿童
LLLB 不面向 13 岁以下儿童。App Store 分级为 9+，面向一般用户。

### 数据删除
删除 App 即可清除所有本机数据。要清除 iCloud 同步的进度，请退出 iCloud
或在"**设置 → Apple ID → iCloud → 管理账户存储**"中删除本 App 的
iCloud 数据。

### 政策变更
我们可能更新本政策。更新后继续使用即视为接受新政策。

### 联系方式
问题反馈：**sduzfan@gmail.com**
